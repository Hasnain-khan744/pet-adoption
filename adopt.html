How to Adopt a Pet

Bringing a new furry friend into your home is a wonderful journey. Follow these simple steps!

Adoption Process

  1. Browse our available pets on the Our Pets page.
  2. Click the "Adopt Me" button on your favorite pet's profile.
  3. Fill out our brief online adoption questionnaire.
  4. Schedule a meet-and-greet at our Happy Paws shelter.
